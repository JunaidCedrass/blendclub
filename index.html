Questions

Straight answers.

What days can I pick up?+
Choose home pickup, Friday collection, or delivery when you place your order.
When do orders close?+
Orders close at the weekly cutoff shown when you order. This gives us time to blend everything fresh.
How do I pay?+
Cash at pickup or EFT before collection. EFT orders open WhatsApp with your order details so you can send proof of payment directly.
What does the protein add-on include?+
A full scoop of Biogen Premium Iso-Whey in your choice of Strawberry or Vanilla. Chocolate is coming soon. Adds roughly 18-21g of protein to your smoothie.
Are the smoothies made fresh?+
Yes. Every smoothie is blended the morning of pickup and kept cold until collection. No pre-made batches sitting overnight.
What if I have allergies?+
Every smoothie lists its allergens in the detail view. Tap any blend to see the full ingredient and allergen breakdown before ordering.
