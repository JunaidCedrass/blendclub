Sunday midnight for Tuesday pickup. Thursday midnight for Saturday. No late orders accepted.
How do I pay?+
Cash at pickup or EFT before collection. EFT orders open WhatsApp with your order details so you can send proof of payment directly.

A full scoop of Biogen Premium Iso-Whey in your choice of Chocolate or Strawberry. Adds roughly 18-21g of protein to your smoothie.

Yes. Every smoothie is blended the morning of pickup and kept cold until collection. No pre-made batches sitting overnight.
